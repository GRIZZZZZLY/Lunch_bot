A system for group lunch chaos, contained.

Rocket Lunch is a Telegram Mini App for deciding what a work chat eats. This is its visual backbone — tokens, primitives, and chrome — shown in both dark and light modes side-by-side so nothing drifts. Mobile-first, 375×667 base, max 430px. Warm peach + lavender + pastel food tones.
